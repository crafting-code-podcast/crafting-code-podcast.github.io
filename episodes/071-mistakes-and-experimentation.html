Mistakes and failures are a common part of software development. But the consequences depend greatly on whether the failure was part of an experiment or if it was a production incident. In this episode, Dave and Allan discuss how we approach failures and what we can do to help us prevent future mistakes. We also discuss the importance of experimentation, and the necessary components to make it safe to experiment.
