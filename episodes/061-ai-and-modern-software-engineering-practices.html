Practices co-evolve with activities. As we (as in industry) increasingly use AI tools, how will this change our existing practices and what new ones will emerge? In this episode, Allan and Dave discuss practices that we've found valuable and what our experiences have been like incorporating AI into our work thus far. We cover some tried-and-true practices that seem to pair well with AI and speculate about changes we might see in others.
