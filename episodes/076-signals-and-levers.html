In this episode, Dave and Allan had the wonderful opportunity to chat with the authors of Signals and Levers: Elisabeth Hendrickson and Joel Tosi! Why is it that we get so caught up in the three illusions of predictability, progress, and control? What levers can the average developer reach? Why do we like causal diagrams so much? How does framing change as a hypothesis affect our mindset? It turns out that software engineers might have more capability for influence than we sometimes think.
