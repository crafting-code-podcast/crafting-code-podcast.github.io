Shift left is a strategy for moving tasks earlier in your workflow or timeline so that issues are discovered and addressed cheaper and faster. We've seen it move through the industry across QA, Operations, and Security. In this episode, Dave and Allan talk about how these shifts have affected our industry and what's happened with those traditional roles. Is the next step for developers begin to shift left into Product roles? Are there some things which should shift right?
